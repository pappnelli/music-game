The Vinyl Vixens

The disc

Fine vinyl grooves, a real record label with a punched spindle hole, and a diagonal light/dark split painted onto the surface -- like the token's two halves, with a bright white seam right on the split line -- instead of a glow. The split is what makes the spin readable, since plain concentric rings look identical at every angle. The shadow lives on the outer static ring; only the groove face underneath it turns, so the drop shadow never swings around.

Shadow system unified

One rule, no exceptions: every surface gets a flat, colored 3D press shadow. There is no glow/blur shadow anywhere in the app anymore — "selected" or "current" states are shown with a colored border plus a stronger tinted press shadow, never a soft halo.

Resting · neutral border shadow
Selected · primary border + tinted shadow
Pressed · shadow collapses to 0

Boxes, unified: a bordered panel (.card) is the only "box" a screen needs. Rows/items living inside one (team rows, radio options, team list items) stay flat at rest — border + tinted background, no shadow of their own — so they don't fight the panel's shadow. Shadow is added back only for two reasons: it's a button (.action-tile always has one), or it's the one row that matters right now (.active team, .selected teammate). Standalone panels that aren't a list of rows -- .round-winner, .qr-panel, the hero .winner-card -- keep their own shadow since nothing contains them. Dead box variants from earlier drafts (old team/medal rows, the compact timeline strip) have been removed so there's one component per job.

Screen background

One smooth diagonal gradient through the brand palette (violet → cyan → hero pink) over the theme's base background, plus a handful of big, low-opacity, gently animated icons -- nothing else. Same recipe as the real Home screen's decorative layer. One shared class, .app-bg (+ .app-bg-icons), drives every screen below -- change it once, every screen updates.
